# NurseNav — Roadmap to Launch

**Project:** NurseNav web application  
**Goal:** Help internationally educated nurses understand and track their journey toward U.S. nursing registration.  
**Launch approach:** Start with a Kenya-focused web app, hosted on Vercel, with individual accounts and M-Pesa payments.

## What the first version will include

- A public website explaining NurseNav and its benefits.
- Secure sign-up and login for each nurse.
- A personal profile and guided onboarding questions.
- A nursing pathway with checklists, document tracking, resources, and saved progress.
- Free introductory content and premium features unlocked after payment.
- M-Pesa checkout and automatic activation of paid access after confirmed payment.
- An admin dashboard for managing users, content, payments, and support.

## Step-by-step launch plan

| Phase | What will happen | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Planning and approval** | Agree on the brand, target audience, app features, pricing, and duration of paid access. | Approved scope and project plan. |
| **2. Design** | Design the main website, sign-up flow, nurse dashboard, pathway pages, and payment screens. | Approved design and user journey. |
| **3. App setup** | Set up the codebase, hosting on Vercel, database, and secure user accounts. | Working app foundation with sign-up and login. |
| **4. Core features** | Build nurse profiles, personalized checklists, learning resources, document-status tracking, and saved progress. | Nurses can use and return to their own dashboards. |
| **5. M-Pesa payments** | Connect M-Pesa, test payments, and ensure premium features unlock only after a successful, verified payment. | Working payment and premium-access system. |
| **6. Admin tools** | Create a private admin area for user management, payment reviews, and content updates. | The business can manage day-to-day operations. |
| **7. Testing and review** | Test the app on phones and computers, check security, verify nursing information, and collect feedback from a small group of nurses. | Launch-ready app with critical issues resolved. |
| **8. Go live** | Connect the final domain, enable live M-Pesa payments, publish policies and support details, and launch to customers. | NurseNav is available to the public. |

## Estimated timeline

**Approximately 10–16 weeks** for an initial release, subject to final scope, feedback, content approval, and M-Pesa production onboarding. The final schedule will be agreed after Phase 1.

## What we will need from the client

- Confirmation of the final product name and branding.
- Approval of the features and which ones are free versus paid.
- Final price in Kenyan shillings and the paid-access period (for example, 12 months).
- Official nursing guidance, existing learning materials, and someone to review accuracy.
- The business details needed for M-Pesa merchant/API activation.
- Approval of the domain, legal pages (privacy, terms, refund policy), and support contact.
- Timely feedback and sign-off at key milestones.

## How we will know the app is ready

A new user can **create an account → complete their profile → view their pathway → save progress → pay using M-Pesa → access premium tools → return later and continue where they left off**.

The administrator can independently review users, payments, and content. The app also passes essential mobile, security, and payment-failure tests.
